# HR process: probation

Duration: 1 month from the worked-in assessment report. Criteria: the
measurable success criteria from the hiring conversation (they are in the
personnel file under "Mandate & Responsibility").

## Weekly check (5 minutes, supervisor + HR colleague)
Record in the colleague's journal:
    ## [yyyy-mm-dd] Probation check week <n>
    - Criterion 1: <on track? yes/no + 1 sentence>
    - Criterion 2: …
    - Correction effort this week: <lower/same/higher>
    - New rules adopted into the personnel file: <count + short list>
    - Outside the assignment: <count + sorting per `hr/feedback-review.md`
      step 3>

The feedback review only runs for active colleagues, so this line is what
evaluates `Outside the assignment:` journal entries during probation.

## Decision at the end (the supervisor decides)
- **active** — criteria met, the collaboration is a noticeable relief.
  → `status: active` in the personnel file, the feedback review rhythm
  begins.
- **extend** (once, +1 month) — clear progress, but criteria not yet met.
  Reasoning goes into the journal.
- **retire** — the rule of thumb: if after a month the collaboration
  brings no relief and is no fun, let them go.
  `status: retired`; personnel file and memory stay in the repo (rehiring
  with a better cut is explicitly allowed and cheap).
