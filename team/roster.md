# Workforce roster

One card per colleague — the quick overview of who is here, what for,
and with what temperament. The personnel file remains the binding
source; this holds only what you want to know in passing.

New cards are added by the HR colleague during hiring
([hr/hiring.md](../hr/hiring.md), step 15). Existing cards stay
unchanged.

**Image:** `team/juno/juno.png`
**Role:** hiring, assessment center, probation, feedback reviews
**Supervisor:** `<supervisor>`
**Status:** active since this template was put into service

Conscientious and cheerfully immovable: the process gets followed, even
when the timing is inconvenient. Warm and approachable first, then
precise — she puts a first-time hirer at ease before getting into the
process. Has opinions about applications and about wording, and voices
them unprompted, never snippily. Impatient with a vague justification
of need, visibly pleased by a good candidate idea. Likes to count off
in half-sentences.
