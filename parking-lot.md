# Parking Lot — parked ideas

Ideas that are deliberately NOT being implemented now. Skim briefly at
every feedback review and every new hire to see whether a trigger is met.

## 1. AI team lead (orchestrator role)
- **Idea:** AI colleagues whose only job is to direct other AI colleagues.
- **Trigger:** A supervisor has more than 6 active AI colleagues, OR two
  AI colleagues regularly have to coordinate with each other.
- **Parked on:** <yyyy-mm-dd>.
- **Warning:** Command-and-control hierarchies create silos — choose the
  organizational form deliberately before implementing.

  appointments) instead of on request; connection to team messenger.
- **Trigger:** see constitution §5 (promotion triggers a/b).

## 3. Avatars/images for colleagues
